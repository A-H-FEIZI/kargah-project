The Project
What is Kargah?

A marketplace connecting Iranian craft masters with people who want to learn from them.

Iran has thousands of traditional craftspeople — carpet weavers, miniature painters, potters, calligraphers, woodcarvers — who carry skills passed down through generations. Most have no digital presence. On the other side, there are people who genuinely want to learn these crafts, but have no way to find a master near them.

Kargah bridges this gap. Masters list their sessions. Seekers browse, filter by city and craft, and request bookings — all through a web platform you will build yourself, from the database to the interface.

🎨

The Master (Ustad)

A craftsperson who lists their sessions — type, price, location — and receives booking requests from learners.

🔍

The Seeker

Someone who browses masters by city and craft, views profiles, and submits a booking request for a session.

The Kargah Model — Three Session Types

What makes Kargah distinct is that masters can offer three different ways to connect with learners:

🏛️

Studio

The seeker visits the master's workshop or studio for in-person instruction.

🚗

Mobile (سیار)

The master travels to the seeker's location — ideal for learners who cannot travel.

📦

Commission

The seeker orders a custom handmade piece directly from the master.

What You Will Build

A complete, working web product — not a tutorial exercise.

Master listing page — browse all masters with filters by city, craft, and session type

Session browser — explore available sessions with type-based tabs

Master profile page — bio, sessions offered, and a booking request form

Booking requests — seekers submit requests; data is saved to a real database

Admin dashboard — add and manage masters, sessions, and booking requests

JWT authentication — admin login protected by industry-standard tokens

Automated tests — 5 API endpoint tests written with pytest

Live deployment — a real public URL you can share with employers
